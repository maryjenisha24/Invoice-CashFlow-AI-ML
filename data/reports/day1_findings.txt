
DAY 1 - UNIVARIATE ANALYSIS FINDINGS

Dataset:
Total invoices: 48,839
Paid invoices: 39,158
Outstanding invoices: 9,681

1. INVOICE AMOUNT
Mean: 32,318.93
Median: 17,559.64
Minimum: 0.72
Maximum: 668,593.36

Finding:
Invoice amounts show considerable variation.
Some high-value invoices may significantly affect cash flow.

2. DAYS TO PAY
Mean: 18.06 days
Median: 15 days
Minimum: 0 days
Maximum: 205 days
Potential outliers: 4,967 (12.68%)

Finding:
Most paid invoices are settled relatively quickly,
but some invoices take much longer to settle.

3. PAYMENT DELAY
Mean: 0.84 days
Median: 0 days
Minimum: -89 days
Maximum: 204 days
Potential outliers: 2,981 (7.61%)

Finding:
Typical payment occurs around the due date.
However, some customers pay significantly early or late.

CONCLUSION:
Invoice amount, days to pay and payment delay
show useful patterns for further analysis.

These variables may help build future payment
delay prediction and customer risk models.
